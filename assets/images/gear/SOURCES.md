# Gear image sources

Product imagery downloaded September 28, 2026. Images are representative catalog colors, not claims about the owner's exact color or configuration. Product images belong to their respective brands.

- `aula-f75.webp`: [EPOMAKER / AULA F75](https://epomaker.com/products/epomaker-aula-f75), catalog asset `AULA_F75_02.png`.
- `jbl-charge-6.webp`: [JBL Charge 6](https://www.jbl.com/CHARGE-6.html), black hero image.
- `jbl-wave-buds-2.webp`: [JBL Wave Buds 2](https://global.jbl.com/wireless-earbuds/WAVE-BUDS-2.html), black hero image.
- `asus-tuf-m4-air.webp`: [ASUS TUF Gaming M4 Air](https://www.asus.com/tr/accessories/mice-and-mouse-pads/tuf-gaming/tuf-gaming-m4-air/), product hero image.
- `iphone-16-pro.webp`: [Apple iPhone 16 Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121031), Apple Store `iphone-16-pro-deserttitanium-select` image.
- `asus-tuf.webp`: user-provided `Laptop.png`, trimmed and optimized to a transparent 1000 × 750 WebP at quality 85. No specific laptop model is inferred.

The five catalog images preserve source transparency and are trimmed, contained in 640 × 480 canvases, and encoded as WebP at quality 82. Exact source URLs and optional regeneration instructions are in `scripts/prepare-gear-images.cjs`. Normal builds do not require its optional Sharp dependency or network access.
